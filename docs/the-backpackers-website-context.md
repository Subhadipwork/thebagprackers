# The Backpackers — Website & Brand Context

Base context for the design, development, branding and content of The Backpackers website.

Logo: [`public/the-backpackers-logo.jpg`](../public/the-backpackers-logo.jpg)

## Brand assets (from the logo)

- **Wordmark:** "The" in a dark-green script, "Backpackers" in bold dark-green sans-serif. The "B" holds a hiker on a mountain against an orange sunset, and the "a" in "packers" is drawn as an orange tent.
- **Motifs:** a plane with a dashed flight path ending at an orange map pin.
- **Tagline lockup:** "WANDER MORE, WORRY LESS." in spaced capitals between orange rules, with an orange swoosh below.
- **Colours (approximate, sampled by eye):** deep forest green `#1F3A2B`, sunset orange `#F7941D`, golden yellow `#FBB040`, off-white background `#FBF8F1`.

## 1. Business overview

| | |
|---|---|
| Business name | The Backpackers |
| Business type | Travel company |
| Base location | Kolkata, West Bengal, India |
| Specialization | Mountain, hill-side and nature-focused travel experiences |
| Tagline | "Wander More, Worry Less." |

The Backpackers is a Kolkata-based travel company focused mainly on trips to scenic mountain and hill destinations. Current destination focus:

- Meghalaya
- Arunachal Pradesh
- Kashmir

The company may expand to other destinations in the future.

## 2. Travel services and tour models

The Backpackers offers **both**:

1. **Group tours:** trips with planned or fixed departure dates that travellers can join.
2. **Customized tours:** trips planned around an individual's or a private group's preferences.

Detailed tour packages, itineraries, prices, inclusions and departure schedules are **not finalized yet**. Website content must not invent these details.

## 3. Brand positioning

The brand should feel:

- Friendly and approachable
- Travel-focused and adventurous
- Connected to mountains, nature and exploration
- Trustworthy and clear
- Welcoming to both group travellers and people who want customized trips

Core message: The Backpackers helps people step away from their everyday routines and discover memorable places and experiences.

Suggested brand message: *"Discover mountains, experience nature, and create memories."*

Supporting lines: "Explore. Experience. Escape." · "Your journey to the mountains starts here."

## 4. Website goals

The first version of the website should:

- Introduce The Backpackers and explain what the company does.
- Establish it as a Kolkata-based travel company.
- Highlight its mountain and hill-destination focus.
- Present the key destinations clearly.
- Explain that both group and customized tours are available.
- Encourage visitors to enquire about trips.
- Provide clear contact and social-media options.

The first version is an **informational, enquiry-generating website**. Online booking and payments are not a confirmed requirement.

### 5.2 About Us

Sections: our story, our travel philosophy, what makes us different.

Draft introduction:

> The Backpackers is a Kolkata-based travel company dedicated to creating memorable travel experiences for people who love mountains, nature, adventure, and exploration.
>
> We focus on trips to some of India's most beautiful mountain destinations, including Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh, and Kashmir. Whether you want to join a group tour or plan a customized journey, we aim to help you discover places and experiences that stay with you long after the trip ends.

Longer brand story (for About or Home intro):

> Our passion is to help travellers escape their everyday routines and discover extraordinary places. Whether it's exploring hidden mountain villages, witnessing breathtaking landscapes, experiencing local cultures, or enjoying peaceful moments surrounded by nature, we aim to make every journey special.

### 5.3 Destinations

Destination cards (each with a "Read More" link) or detail pages:

- **Meghalaya:** Discover Meghalaya's green hills, dramatic landscapes, waterfalls, caves, and scenic natural surroundings.
- **Arunachal Pradesh:** Explore the mountain landscapes, valleys, distinctive local culture, and peaceful destinations of Arunachal Pradesh.
- **Kashmir:** Experience Kashmir's majestic mountains, valleys, lakes, and changing seasonal landscapes.
- **Other destinations:** placeholder for future expansion.

These are introductory teasers only. Add specific attractions, routes, durations and seasonal claims only after the company confirms the destinations and itineraries it runs.

### 5.4 Tours / Explore Trips

Explain the two tour formats:

- **Group Tours:** Join a planned trip with other travellers. Departure dates and package details will be published when finalized.
- **Customized Tours:** Tell us your preferred destination, dates, group size and travel preferences, and we'll plan a trip that suits you.

Later, the packages page can grow to include destination-wise packages, trip duration, highlights, inclusions/exclusions and enquiry/booking options.

For launch, use an **"Explore Trips"** or **"Enquire About a Trip"** call to action. Do not publish unconfirmed prices, dates or inclusions.

### 5.5 Why Travel With Us?

Communicate the approach without unsupported claims:

- Kolkata-based travel company
- Focus on mountain and nature destinations
- Group and customized trip options
- Enquiry-led trip planning
- A focus on memorable travel experiences

Add claims about years of experience, safety certifications, local teams, 24/7 support or customer numbers **only if the business can verify them**.

### 5.6 Travel Gallery

- Destination photographs
- Landscapes and scenic views
- Moments from completed trips
- Traveller experiences (with permission)

Use authentic company photographs wherever possible, and clearly separate illustrative destination images from photos of the company's own trips.

Use these consistently:

- Explore Trips
- Plan Your Trip

## 8. Content and accuracy guidelines

- Write all public-facing content in English.
- Keep the tone friendly, clear and inviting, not heavy marketing.
- Describe The Backpackers as Kolkata-based.
- Keep the main focus on mountain, hill-side and nature travel.
- Mention Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh and Kashmir as current areas of focus.
- State clearly that both group and customized tours are offered.
- **Do not invent** package names, prices, departure dates, itineraries, inclusions, customer reviews, company history or operational guarantees.
- Treat detailed packages as future content until finalized.
- Use genuine contact details and authentic trip photos once provided.
